Corrections the research forced

Building the brief’s programme list verbatim would have created programmes PDEU does not run.

  • not a programme
    B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering CSE is the department. The programme it runs is Computer Engineering. The same department also runs CSBS, which the brief did not list and whose structure differs materially.
  • not offered
    B.Tech Automobile Engineering Zero occurrences in the current admission policy. It survives only as 24ME437T, a Program Elective 5 option inside Mechanical Engineering.
  • wrong school
    Electrical and Chemical are School of Energy Technology Not School of Technology — which matters, because Open Elective eligibility is written in schools. Two further SoET programmes the brief omitted (Petroleum, Biotechnology) were added.

The pre-existing seed’s B.TECH-CSE — “B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering” was therefore not a real PDEU programme name. It has been replaced rather than carried forward.

03 Versioning

The admission batch is part of the key

ECE has two published curricula, and they are not revisions of one another. Two ECE students in the same lecture hall can therefore be governed by different rules — which is why resolution goes through the batch window rather than through department and semester.

09 Uncertainty

Eleven things that were flagged instead of guessed

Six components carry REQUIRES_FACULTY_CONFIRMATION, and a curriculum version cannot be published while any remain.

  1. Climate Science & Sustainable Engineering
    Admitting 60 students for 2026–27, but no curriculum published anywhere. The programme was not created.
  2. Biotechnology Semesters III–VIII
    The official structure stops at Semester II. Imported as a draft; reaches no student.
  3. Biotechnology’s social internship sits in Semester II
    Unlike every other programme. Recorded from source and flagged as a conflict, not silently moved.
  4. Chemical prints “Program Elective 3” twice
    Once in Sem VI and again in Sem VII — almost certainly a typo for PE4. Imported verbatim with a conflict note rather than quietly “fixed”.
  5. Minor allocation “pro rata basis”
    The rules never define the pro-rata denominator. CPI ranking implemented; pro-rata left configurable and unset.
  6. Mechanical and Chemical Sem IV Open Elective
    Listed as a named departmental course. Modelled as the department’s basket contribution, flagged.
  7. CSBS Open Electives are prescribed
    Not a cross-school basket. DIRECT selection, flagged.
  8. Electrical has no published 2024–28 structure
    Only 2020–24 exists on the university host. No 2024 curriculum was invented.
  9. Division counts
    No official source states them. Derived from seat intake, labelled DERIVED_FROM_INTAKE, and editable.
  10. Open Elective 40-minimum and 75-per-section
    Departmental, not public. Left entirely unchanged.
  11. The pre-existing ICT 2022-R1 curriculum
    Has no public document. Retained for its cohort, flagged with review notes explaining why.

11 API

A publication lifecycle with real refusals

RouteBehaviour
GET /curriculumstatus, batch label, source provenance, flagged count
GET /curriculum/meresolves through the batch window, published versions only
GET /curriculum/programs/{id}/divisionsdivisions by batch, with student counts
POST /curriculum/versionscreate a version — always begins as a draft
POST /curriculum/{id}/revisecopy into a new draft; closes the previous batch window so the two never overlap
POST /curriculum/{id}/publishrefused while any component needs faculty confirmation
POST /curriculum/{id}/unpublishrefused while students are governed by it
POST /curriculum/{id}/archiverefused while students are governed by it
POST /curriculum/{id}/componentsrefused on a published version — revise instead
POST /curriculum/components/{id}/confirmclear a flag — deliberate, audited, by a named person
DELETE /curriculum/divisions/{id}refused while students are assigned to it

All management routes require academics:manage; every mutation writes an audit row. Verified live: publishing a flagged draft, unpublishing a live version, archiving a governed version and editing a published curriculum are all refused with an explanation, while revise → publish succeeds.

15 Limitations

What is deliberately missing, and why

  1. Biotechnology is incomplete
    Only Semesters I–II exist publicly. It stays a draft and serves no student until the department supplies the rest.
  2. No Electrical 2024–28 curriculum
    Only the 2020–24 structure is published, so Electrical’s 2024+ batches have none. Deliberate — the alternative was inventing one.
  3. Climate Science & Sustainable Engineering is absent
    Despite admitting students, for the same reason.
  4. Division counts are derived, not cited
    Labelled as such in the data, and editable from the admin screen.
  5. No Semester V+ cohort on a 2024–28 curriculum
    Those curricula begin with the 2024 batch, which is genuinely only in Semester IV in AY 2025–26. Later-semester screens are exercised by the ICT and 2020-scheme cohorts instead; the alternative was placing students on curricula that do not cover them.
  6. Industrial Orientation has no internship programme
    It is modelled as a curriculum component, but no official source describes a document lifecycle for a three-week, zero-credit orientation.
  7. The running Docker backend image is stale
    docker compose up --build is needed to serve this code. The database is already migrated and seeded.
